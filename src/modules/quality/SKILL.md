# Quality Domain Contract

## Purpose

The quality module manages product and process assurance, hold/release decisions, NCRs, disposition, and traceability. It must preserve evidence and support governed release decisions.

## Core Responsibilities

- inspections and sampling
- quality hold and quarantine management
- NCR and disposition workflow
- rework and scrap handling
- lot-level traceability and release status

## Mandatory Rules

1. Quality holds must prevent unauthorized use or release of affected material.
2. Disposition decisions must be explicitly reasoned and auditable.
3. Scrap and rework actions require recording of cause and owner.
4. Release status is a first-class state tied to product and lot integrity.
5. Quality decisions must be consistent with inventory and workflow boundaries.

## Domain Boundaries

The quality module coordinates with inventory, procurement, engineering, and workflow, but it must not assume control of unrelated business operations.
